# no /etc/passwd, so the lane supplies the sandssh fakepwd shim through
# SANDSSH_PASSWD and LD_PRELOAD. A normal host sets neither and the same cases

# `interop/` rows are against the sandssh tree unchanged -- its relay under
# podssh on both ends, its client against podssh's relay and server, and its
# server under podssh's relay and client. A protocol that only talks to itself
# is a fork of sandssh, not a replacement for it.
