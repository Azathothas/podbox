#    no libc `statx` entry point is called that the interposer could map.
# 2. strace -f of the BEFORE binary, `node -e` with readFileSync, statSync

#    `statx` (ENOENT); `fs.promises.open` and `mkdir` go through the

#    io_uring. A ring could carry IORING_OP_STATX or IORING_OP_OPENAT,
#    which the table never sees, so the fix refuses io_uring_setup too.
