it encodes the stale anchor, its plant passes vacuously — that is exactly why
wave 0 is first.

⛔ **`120-reproducible-build.sh` and `157` are one binary because both are

`scripts/check-todo.py:228` names it in `CEILING_SCRIPT`; and
`scripts/plant.sh:191-193` reads `CEILING_BYTES` out of it with

three. Repair every one in the same commit, or the gate is broken on merge.
