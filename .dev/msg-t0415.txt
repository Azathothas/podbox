complete: name what a device shim displaced (T-0415)

An image shipping a directory, symlink or fifo where a device node
belongs got a silent outcome: the directory failed inside an unnamed
row, and the replacements read exactly like fresh shims. The fixup now
reads each path's shape before asking the kernel. Directories become
named Failed rows, links come out before the mknod attempt so no node
can land outside the rootfs, and every replacement names what it
displaced. The clean paths read byte-identical rows.

Three unit tests pin the directory, symlink and fifo shapes with the
devices suite at 47 of 47, and the full lane check is green. The 240
sweep exits 0 at 10 of 10 built and ran with every device row
identical; one row's mirror-dependent lines dropped out and returned
on a re-drive, which is the live mirror probe answering per run.
