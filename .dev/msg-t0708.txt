errors the runtime already understands, and most stopped instead of
adapting. Each now runs as a counted emulation: a regular file, a tally
chain plus success, success plus a memo, stripped flags before the real
call. inspect carries the tally and the banner states the tier.

green beside it. The lane check is green with the interpose suite at 25
of 25.
